- **Web research:** require loaded providers registering `web_search`, `fetch_content`, `get_search_content`, and `source_check`. Global `pi-web-access` installation alone is insufficient: use background ambient extension discovery, or parent-configured `extensions`/`subagentOnlyExtensions` for foreground children. Do not assume foreground children inherit ambient extensions. Fetched-source inspection may explain a registered `source_check` call failure, not substitute for missing registration.
